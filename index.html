01 — THE UNEXPECTED BEGINNING
Before you became my person

We were course mates.
Strangers, somehow.

We had been in the same academic world, sharing the same course, crossing through the same semester — yet we hadn't really met. Then one day, a group assignment put us in the same moment.

It didn't look like the beginning of anything extraordinary. Just classmates, an assignment, and a conversation that could easily have been an ordinary one.

But life can hide a whole chapter inside a very ordinary day.

02 — THE NOTES
A small kindness I still remember

I asked for your notes.

I hadn't copied the notes that semester. I was busy building Akrada, trying to make something real, and I still had exams to prepare for. So I asked if I could have your notes to snap them — and you let me.

A page from the beginning

There I was, looking for something to study from…

…and unknowingly receiving a first page of us.

At the time, I was thinking about the exams ahead. I didn't know that one day, I would look back at that simple exchange and see how much it meant.

03 — THE NUMBER
A contact saved. A door opened.
♡

Then we exchanged numbers.

It was practical. Natural. Nothing dramatic — just a way to reach each other after that assignment and the notes.

And then we went home. The day could have ended there, filed away with every other day at school.

You texted me that your bag had been stolen — with your books and assignments inside.

I remember telling you to stay calm, that God knows best, and that maybe it had happened for a reason.

04 — WHAT WE COULDN'T HAVE PLANNED
A moment that became part of our story

Neither of us knew what was happening.

We didn't sit down that day and decide that we would become important to each other. We didn't know how a simple conversation would turn into something this meaningful.

We just kept talking. Somehow, little by little, two people who had barely known each other found themselves falling in love.

What I wish I could tell that version of us

You had no idea what this little exchange would grow into.

And maybe that's the beautiful part.

Alhamdulillah for the way our paths crossed.

05 — THE THING I ADORE
Not a perfect version. The real you.

I adore that you're real.

Not because you have to be polished every second. Not because you have to say the perfect thing, look a certain way, or pretend to be someone else.

I adore the realness in you — the person you are when there's no performance to keep up, no mask to wear, no need to be anything other than yourself.

There's something precious about being able to care for someone as they truly are. And I hope you always feel that you can be fully yourself with me: honest, human, still growing, and loved.

You don't have to become a different person to be worthy of tenderness. The real you is the one I want to keep knowing.

06 — THE UNWRITTEN PAGES
No pretending to know the future

There is still so much story left.

More conversations, more ordinary days that might become treasured memories, more lessons in understanding each other, more reasons to be grateful.

I pray that Allah guides us toward what is good, grows kindness and patience between us, and — if He wills — lets our story find its way to Nikkah with barakah in it.

Not because life must be perfect to be beautiful, but because whatever we build deserves sincerity, respect, and the care to keep choosing what is right.

07 — FROM ME TO YOU
Read this slowly, my Queen

My Sarauniya,

When I think about us, I don't only think about love as a big word. I think about that group assignment. Your notes. The number we exchanged. Your message when you got home. A conversation that neither of us knew would become the beginning of something so dear to us.

And I think about how unexpectedly someone can become a part of your heart. You came into my life without a grand entrance, but your presence has become something I genuinely value.

I adore you for being real. I don't want to love an imagined version of you. I want to keep learning the person you are, listening to you, laughing with you, being honest with you, and treating your heart with care.

Thank you for being you, Sarauniya. Thank you for the day our paths finally met, even though we had been course mates all along. Of all the things that day could have brought, I'm grateful it brought me the chance to know you.

May Allah bless you, protect your heart, make ease find you, and guide every step ahead. And if Allah writes that we are to build a life together, may He make it gentle, faithful, and full of barakah.

Alhamdulillah — for the notes, the number, the first message, and the love we never saw coming.

Yours, with sincerity,
Muhammad ♡
